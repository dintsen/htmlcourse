## What it is
Kyoto tea house: origins 1717 (Rihei Watanabe's shop Omiya, tea and ceramics), renamed Ippodo in 1846, "preserve one" (about page text). Sells matcha, gyokuro, sencha, teabags, utensils, in cylindrical cans with boxes, pouches and seasonal limited boxes (e.g. Tsukikage autumn matcha).

## Current site
Standard Shopify theme: pale grey/white, Cormorant-like serif nav, mustard torn-paper strip, "visiting from USA" popup, small search pill, icon row. The gouache hero is the only moment with soul; the product pages are catalogue grids. Colour palette unused: matcha green, mustard paper, indigo, hanko orange.

## What the brand owns
Three centuries of continuity, kanji labels, tin cans, paper-and-ink illustration, matcha green, the ritual (powder, whisk, pour).

## Opportunity
A slow, tactile experience: tin cans with label wraps, pour/steep as a motion language, illustrated Kyoto scene as navigation, bilingual typography (kanji + Latin serif). Opposite pole of a black tech page.

## Risks
- Image set is mostly pale-background packshots; real cut-outs need processing. Many can shots are only 1000px.
- Illustrators credited on site (rights belong to them; fine for a non-commercial concept, flag it).
- Locale popup / USA redirect; Japanese brand names must be copied exactly.
- No official 3D, no brand guidelines.

## Hunch
A steeping-time scroll: a single can opens into matcha powder, the illustrated street becomes the menu, calligraphic mincho type on paper.
