# Analogue (analogue.co) — FPGA retro handhelds and consoles

Category: consumer electronics / retro gaming hardware. Physical product, not a household name (known to retro-game and design audiences).

## Evidence
- Opened live (inspect-ref contact sheet viewed). Next.js site, no WebGL, no libs; fonts Circular + Unica77 Mono + NeueBit (all commercial; legal substitutes needed).
- Public press kit: https://www.analogue.co/press with per-product folders. 107 downloadable images, direct URLs of the form https://www.analogue.co/press/item/5-Analogue%20Pocket/3-Analogue%20Pocket%20All%20Angles.jpg. Folders: Analogue 3D, Pocket, Pocket Transparent / Glow-in-the-Dark / Classic / Aluminum limited editions, Duo, Mega Sg, Super Nt.
- Sampled 8 press files, all hi-res: 3000x2000 to 6000x3541, up to 5000x5000 and 4096x4096 (Pocket hardcase exploded view, all-angle Pocket sheet, transparent family, glow-in-the-dark macro, Mega Sg + Sonic). Clean CGI renders, art-directed backgrounds (black, mint, cobalt, white).
- Home page harvest: images.analogue.co serves 8000px-wide PNG/JPG renders (Pocket + Dock, Analogue 3D with cartridges, Supreme Pocket).
- Vector: /icon.svg (the "A" mark, 90x90 path) plus inline product wordmark SVGs. No full brand-guidelines document found.
- No official GLB/USDZ/AR model, no video found. 3D would be rebuilt: rounded slab, d-pad, buttons, cartridge slot and dock are simple enough to model credibly, and the exploded-view renders are a reference.

## Current site
Quiet grey/white Next.js page: tiny type, a small product hero floating in flat grey, a Supreme collab strip, a review-quotes strip, black footer. Wastes the materials (translucent shells, glow-in-the-dark, anodised aluminium, saturated Classic colours) and the pixel-font culture. Brand owns: matte black + translucent plastics, pixel type, cartridge ritual, "respect for game history" tone.

## Risks
- Renders show third-party game art on screens/cartridges (Kirby, GoldenEye 007, Sonic, Snow White): crop, swap screens for own pixel art, or avoid.
- Supreme co-brand imagery should not be reused.
- Fonts are commercial; pixel/mono substitutes needed.
- Imagery is all CGI (no photography), so the page needs strong art direction not photo realism.

## Hunch
A shelf of colour: one screen-sized stage where the Pocket shells (clear, glow, classic palette) swap like cartridges and the page itself boots, glows and changes colour with each shell.
