Opened live: yes (inspect-ref + harvest, Chromium, desktop+mobile). URL probed: https://4711.com (Shopify store, English at /en).

## Current site
Generic white Shopify theme: uppercase Jost H1, carousels of packshot cards, newsletter strip, cookie modal (CookieFirst), no WebGL, 13k px page. The one image-led moment is the pale-blue hero panel. The brand's real ownership (turquoise + gold filigree label, the 4711 numerals in an oval, red-orange cap, Cologne cathedral on the box, Remix/Acqua Colonia colour-coded lines, Dufthaus) is almost absent from the UI. Maximum headroom.

## Opportunity
Colour ownership is total (turquoise/gold) and the bottle is a simple rotational glass form with a gold-banded red cap, so a procedural or photo-built 3D bottle is realistic, using the 2160px alpha packshots as texture/reference. Variant system already exists (fragrance families, miniature sets, Remix colourways). Merch (stripes) adds a second visual register.

## Risks
Logo raster only (must be rebuilt from the label art or sourced elsewhere; do not redraw if a vector turns up). Imagery is CGI-style packshot rather than editorial, so mood must be art-directed from composite/procedural work. German-first copy; English store exists. Cloudflare challenge script is loaded but did not block Chromium.

## Hunch
Treat the label as the interface: a turquoise-and-gold ornamental frame that opens onto the bottle, with the cathedral-box silhouette as the navigation motif, instead of another white catalogue grid.
