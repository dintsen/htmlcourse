# SELECTED TEN (ECD decision; 08–10 pending probes of automotive/mobility, sport, fashion/object candidates)

## 01 Playdate (Panic)
- dir: concepts/01-playdate
- category: Consumer technology — handheld game console (physical object)
- why: A one-of-a-kind yellow handheld with a crank: instantly recognisable, simple enough to model convincingly in 3D, official packshots with alpha (13) plus 21 vector logo files, playful brand with a loyal design audience. The crank is a ready-made signature interaction.
- **colour**: Saturated Playdate yellow field + black + warm greys (light/chromatic).
- **type**: Friendly quirky grotesk for the page; 1-bit/pixel type ONLY inside the device screen where it is real UI.
- **layout**: Device-centred; the page behaves like the device's own screen and menu; stepwise rather than free-flowing.
- **density**: Medium, spacious.
- **motion**: Mechanical and quantised: ratchet/tick motion tied to crank rotation; no smooth-scroll glide, no fade-up.
- **threeD**: YES — a convincingly modelled Playdate (Three.js) as the hero object; crank rotates with the visitor's scroll/drag.
- **webgl**: Yes: 3D device with live canvas texture on its screen (1-bit dithered content).
- **photo**: Official product photography + official game art (1-bit); no lifestyle stock.
- **nav**: Crank = scroll/nav; on-device D-pad-style menu.
- **hero**: Device centred, crank visible, screen already alive.
- **seed**: Crank-driven scroll: turning the crank moves through the site; the device screen shows each section as a 1-bit game-like scene.

## 02 Ippodo Tea (Kyoto)
- dir: concepts/02-ippodo
- category: Beverage — premium Japanese tea (physical product, ritual)
- why: Kyoto tea house since 1717 with beautiful tins (16 alpha packshots), painted illustration language and a ritual product (steeping) that suits an interactive liquid/ink experience; very different mood from every other pick.
- **colour**: Washi ivory + deep tea green + sumi ink black + a restrained indigo; light and quiet.
- **type**: Refined text serif + quiet sans; vertical Japanese typography as a layout device where authentic.
- **layout**: Asymmetric, generous negative space, pinned vertical narrative; objects float in whitespace.
- **density**: Very low.
- **motion**: Slow, breath-like, ink diffusion; reveals are wipes/diffusions, never upward fades.
- **threeD**: None (no 3D object).
- **webgl**: YES — fluid/ink-in-water shader for the steeping interaction (liquid simulation, meaningful).
- **photo**: Official tin packshots + painted illustration; macro leaf textures only if sourced from official material.
- **nav**: Vertical side rail with tea categories as an ink-brush index.
- **hero**: A tin in still water; ink blooms as the page loads.
- **seed**: Steep your tea: choose a tea, temperature and time; ink diffuses in real time and the steps (amounts, temperature, time) are the real brewing guide.

## 03 Hasselblad
- dir: concepts/03-hasselblad
- category: Consumer technology — medium-format cameras (physical products, photography)
- why: Swedish medium-format maker with exceptional official imagery up to 5120px, vector logo, and a product (camera + lens) that is also the means of making great images; today's site is conventional and dark, leaving large headroom.
- **colour**: Reverse the cliché: warm paper white / light graphite gallery with the black cameras as objects; a single quiet accent. LIGHT.
- **type**: Wide, confident neo-grotesque/spaced-caps echo of the wordmark with a text serif for captions.
- **layout**: Gallery-wall / contact-sheet composition; images are the grid; asymmetric hangs.
- **density**: Low.
- **motion**: Optical: focus pulls, aperture-like reveals, image-shader transitions; no fade-up.
- **threeD**: None (no 3D camera model).
- **webgl**: YES — image shader transitions/lens effects on official photography.
- **photo**: Official camera/lens product shots + Hasselblad Masters photographs; treat as prints on a wall.
- **nav**: Persistent caption/index bar (exhibition-label style), not a hamburger.
- **hero**: One full-bleed photograph with an aperture/focus reveal.
- **seed**: Expose-through: scrubbing aperture/focus reveals the photograph and then the camera that made it.
